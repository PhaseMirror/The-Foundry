# ADR-030: Citizen Gardens as Governance Prototype

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-04-15
- **Owners:** Lead Multiplicity Theorist
- **Related ADRs:** ADR-027 (Plan), ADR-029 (Floor)
- **Systems:** Citizen Gardens, Phase Mirror (Governance), PIRTM

---

## 1. Context

Phase Mirror was designed as a governance tool for organizations, but it requires a real-world, physical validation of its methodology to demonstrate recursive stability and generalizability. Citizen Gardens is a civic infrastructure project that is not merely governed *by* Phase Mirror — it **is** Phase Mirror operating in the physical, civic, and economic domain.

This ADR formalizes Citizen Gardens as the **primary prototype** for Phase Mirror governance, where the software and the institution operate under the same methodology.

---

## 2. Decision

Citizen Gardens will serve as the living proof-of-concept (POC) for the Phase Mirror Dissonance (PMD) operating loop.

### 2.1 PMD Operating Loop Alignment
The five-step PMD operating loop — **Extract, Map Tensions, Rank, Produce Output, Pose Precision Question** — will serve as the constitutional operating procedure for the Citizen Gardens civic institution.

### 2.2 Shared Governance Substrate
| PMD Layer | Software Context | Citizen Gardens Civic Context |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **L0 Invariants** | Schema hash, permission bits, nonce freshness | Community bylaws (e.g., non-negotiable land use for public benefit) |
| **L1 Rules** | Policy alignment (MD-001 through MD-005) | Designation terms, harvest yield formulas, IP royalty percentages |
| **Dissonance Detection** | Drift between stated policy and actual configuration | Drift between a member's designated contribution and their reward |
| **Circuit Breaker** | Degraded mode when thresholds exceeded | Emergency governance protocols for contested contracts or yield anomalies |

---

## 3. Rationale

### 3.1 Recursive Stability (Proof of Concept)
Citizen Gardens provides the living, observable implementation of Phase Mirror that predates commercial customers. This recursive stability — where the software governs the physical system and the physical system informs the software's calibration — is the ultimate proof of the Phase Mirror methodology's effectiveness.

### 3.2 Strong Positioning for Grants and Partnerships
By framing Citizen Gardens as the physical substrate for a formalized mathematical framework (PIRTM), we establish a complete research arc from theory to implementation to real-world validation. This positioning is exceptionally strong for grants such as the NSF Mathematical Foundations of AI and the Cooperative AI Foundation.

---

## 4. Implementation

- **Charter Encoding**: The most urgent artifact is the formal charter or set of bylaws for Citizen Gardens that explicitly encodes the PMD operating loop as its governance procedure.
- **Federated Replication**: Citizen Gardens serves as the template for other communities to adopt the PMD methodology, becoming nodes in a federated governance network.

---

## 5. Consequences

- **Positive**: 
  - Immediate real-world validation of the methodology.
  - Stronger justification for grant applications and partnerships.
  - Transparent, mathematical governance for the Citizen Gardens community.
- **Negative**: 
  - Governance decisions are no longer discretionary but mathematically defined.
  - Higher initial overhead for codifying physical rules into the L1/L2 policy layers.
