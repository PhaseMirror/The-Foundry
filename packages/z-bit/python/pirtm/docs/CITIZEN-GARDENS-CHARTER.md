# Citizen Gardens Constitutional Charter

## 1. Governance Identity

Citizen Gardens is a living civic infrastructure and the primary prototype for Phase Mirror governance. It operates as a sovereign entity where the software methodology and the institutional operating procedure are unified.

## 2. The PMD Operating Loop

All governance decisions, asset designations, and dispute resolutions within Citizen Gardens MUST follow the five-step Phase Mirror Dissonance (PMD) operating loop:

1. **Extract**: Identify all relevant data, contracts, and stakeholder inputs.
2. **Map Tensions**: Surface all dissonances or conflicts between current state and stated policy.
3. **Rank**: Prioritize tensions based on their impact on the community's L0 invariants.
4. **Produce Output**: Generate a clear, actionable dissonance report.
5. **Pose Precision Question**: Formulate the single, critical question that resolves the tension.

## 3. Civic Invariants (L0)

The following invariants are non-negotiable and cannot be overridden by any governance vote:
- **Public Benefit**: No asset designation can remove land or infrastructure from the public benefit pool.
- **Contractual Yield**: No resource yield (harvest, IP royalty, capital) can flow without a live, verified contract.
- **Due Process**: No member can be excluded from participation or benefits without a formal PMD audit and due process.
- **PIRTM Anchoring**: All digital governance logs must be anchored to the PIRTM invariant floor.

## 4. Designation and Audit

- **Asset Designation**: Land, IP, capital, and labor are designated under L1 rule sets (MD-001 through MD-005).
- **Continuous Audit**: Phase Mirror runs as the dissonance oracle, continuously monitoring the drift between designated contribution and recognized reward.
- **Circuit Breaker**: Emergency governance protocols are triggered automatically if a yield function produces an anomaly or a contract is contested.

## 5. Federation and Replication

Citizen Gardens serves as the root node for a federated network of sovereign communities. Replicating communities must:
1. Adopt the PMD methodology.
2. Designate assets under conformant contracts.
3. Use Phase Mirror as their dissonance oracle.
4. Achieve certification by the Citizen Gardens root node.
