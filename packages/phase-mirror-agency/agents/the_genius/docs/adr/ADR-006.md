# ADR-006: Meta-Controller and Trajectory Steering

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-05-26
- Owners: Multiplicity Foundation / Citizen Gardens
- Tags: meta-control, autonomy, phoenix, steering
- Phase: phase-1
- Published-At: models/the_genius/docs/adr/ADR-006.md

## 1. Context
The "Phase-0 Genius Patterns" report identified clear signatures of healthy vs. unstable trajectories. To maintain stability and optimize exploration, we need a mechanism that can observe these patterns in real-time and act upon them to steer the system away from degenerate regimes.

## 2. Decision
We implement a **Meta-Controller** that provides two levels of intervention:
1. **Soft Guidance (Micro-control):** Sliding-window analysis of loss-norm correlation and PIRTM norms to dynamically adjust ZMODAdam/LM strength and Guardian strictness.
2. **Phoenix Triggering (Macro-control):** Automating ADR-008 resets when the "Veto Trigger" (correlation > 0.6) or emergency norm thresholds are hit.

## 3. Alternatives Considered
- **Fixed Hyperparameter Schedules:** Rejected as they cannot respond to the stochastic and complex dynamics of multiplicity-theoretic optimization.
- **Manual Intervention:** Rejected as it limits the scalability and autonomy of the agiOS agents.

## 4. Dependencies
- ADR-007/009: Governance and Compliance
- ADR-008: Phoenix-ℵ₀ Cycle
- PIRTM Telemetry Stream

## 5. Risks
- **Over-Correction:** Oscillatory behavior if the controller is too aggressive in its adjustments.
- **Complexity:** Adds another layer of feedback loops that must be carefully tuned.

## 5. Implementation & Verification

### 5.1 Target Artifacts
- **Module:** `src/zmod/controller.py`
- **Updated Runner:** `scripts/train_mnist_genius.py` (Meta-Controller integration)

### 5.2 Promotion Criteria (Proposed -> Accepted)
| Criteria Type | Description | Target / Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stability** | Reduction in Veto Trigger events | ≥ 30% reduction |
| **Autonomy** | Automatic Phoenix reset recovery | 100% success rate |

## 6. Consequences
- **Positive:** Increased system robustness and autonomy; faster discovery of stable learning regimes.
- **Negative:** Requires continuous maintenance of "Healthy Band" and "Veto Trigger" definitions based on empirical reports.
