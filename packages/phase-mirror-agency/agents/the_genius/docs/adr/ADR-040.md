## 1. Context
The Riemann zeta function $\zeta(s)$ is intimately connected to the distribution of prime numbers through its non-trivial zeros $\rho_n = 1/2 + i\gamma_n$. We wish to incorporate the "resonance bands" created by these zeros directly into the optimizer's potential landscape. This allows the optimizer to respond to the spectral signatures of the zeros without requiring a full evaluation of $\zeta(s)$ at every point.

## 2. Decision
We implement a **Zero-Resonance Layer** based on tabulated zero ordinates $\{\gamma_n\}_{n=1}^N$.

We define a smooth resonance kernel $R_N(t)$ over the imaginary axis:
$$R_N(t) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^N \exp\left( -\frac{(t - \gamma_n)^2}{2\sigma^2} \right)$$

The auxiliary resonance loss is:
$$L_{zero}(\theta, \pi) = \lambda_{zeros} \cdot \tilde{\phi}(\theta) \cdot R_N(t(\theta, \pi))$$

Where $t(\theta, \pi) = \text{Im } s(\theta, \pi)$ is the imaginary part of the embedding. This term increases the "cost" of the state when the imaginary part (determined by parameter energy and multiplicity) aligns with a Riemann zero.

## 3. Alternatives Considered
- **Analytic Zeta Continuation:** Using a full analytic approximation of $\zeta(s)$ in the critical strip. Rejected as computationally too expensive for high-frequency optimizer updates.
- **Random Resonance Bands:** Using random ordinates instead of Riemann zeros. This is kept as a **control method** for the benchmark matrix (see ADR-050) but rejected as the primary method.

## 4. Dependencies
- ADR-010: Canonical Embedding Specification
- Data: Tabulated Riemann zeros (e.g., from Odlyzko's tables)

## 5. Risks
- **Spectral Leakage:** Using a finite $N$ and a non-zero $\sigma$ blurs the sharp spectral structure of the zeros.
- **Hyperparameter Sensitivity:** The choice of $\sigma$ (bandwidth) and $\lambda_{zeros}$ is critical; too small a $\sigma$ may lead to "missed" resonances, while too large a $\sigma$ washes out the structure.

## 6. Consequences
- **Positive:** Incorporates deep number-theoretic structure into the optimizer; provides a mechanism for "zero-guided" exploration.
- **Negative:** Requires external data (zero tables); adds another auxiliary loss term to manage.
