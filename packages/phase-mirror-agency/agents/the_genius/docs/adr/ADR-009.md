# ADR-009: Constitutional Projector / Validation-First Gate

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-05-26
- Owners: Multiplicity Foundation / Citizen Gardens
- Tags: governance, ace, validation, prime-indexing
- Phase: phase-1
- Published-At: models/the_genius/docs/adr/ADR-009.md

## 1. Context
The Genius v2 trajectory identifies a self-bootstrapping loop where the framework generates new "cells" (conjectures) faster than they can be validated. To prevent "conjecture bloat" and ensure structural integrity, a "Validation-First Gate" is required.

## 2. Decision
We implement a "Constitutional Projector" onto externally verifiable states:
1. **Validation-First Gate:** Each canonical conjecture cell must be backed by at least one reproducible numerical result before new canonical cells may be admitted.
2. **ACE Budget (Absolute Contraction Energy):** We quantify the "lawfulness budget" of the project. High-risk conjectures are capped at 25% of total effort.
3. **Prime-Ordered Closure:** Gaps in the framework are re-ordered into a prime-indexed roadmap (7/30/90-day cycles).

## 3. Alternatives Considered
- **Unconstrained Exploration:** Rejected as it leads to divergence between theory and implementation.
- **Strict Waterfall Validation:** Rejected as it stifles the creative "high-risk" exploration necessary for breakthroughs.

## 4. Dependencies
- ADR-001: Metrics and Evidence Policy
- ACE (Absolute Contraction Energy) definitions

## 5. Risks
- **Stifled Creativity:** Over-enforcement of the gate could kill promising but "hard-to-validate" ideas too early.
- **Complexity of ACE Tracking:** Defining a rigorous "energy" metric for research effort is challenging.
