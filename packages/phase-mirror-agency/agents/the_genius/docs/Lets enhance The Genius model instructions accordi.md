2. Critique of the enhanced version
   From a mathematical and philosophical angle, the enhanced model has several
   vulnerabilities:
   Choice of primes is under-justified
   Listing cognitive generators is easy; proving they are minimal, independent, and sufficient
   is hard.
   Without a principled derivation (e.g., from symmetry, invariance, or a universal property),
   “prime” is just a metaphor.
   State space SSS is underspecified
   What exactly is a “state of understanding”?
   If SSS is not clearly defined (e.g., as a structured space of representations with typed
   morphisms), then pi:S→Sp_i: S \to Spi​:S→S is hand-wavy, not a real morphism in a well-
   defined category.
   Genius field GGG mixes epistemic and pragmatic metrics
   Novelty, coherence, and market success are not commensurate without a carefully justified
   aggregation.
   You risk Goodhart’s law: optimizing for a composed scalar proxy GGG might destroy the
   very thing you want to measure.
   Phase-Mirror adaptation may converge to local minima
   The reflection operator RRR could overfit to near-term gains in GGG, suppressing rare but
   crucial high-risk primes (e.g., wild Perturb moves).
   This contradicts historical genius, which often moves through long stretches of “apparent
   failure” before breakthroughs.
   Genius types as fixed distributions may be too static
   Real inventors shift mode: an “Einstein-type” in physics may be “beginner-type” in
  economics.
  A single global type per person or project is probably inadequate; you need situation-
  dependent types or a hierarchical model.
  Validation problem
  It’s unclear how to falsify the claim that “genius = certain prime patterns.”
  Without predictive constraints (“given early primes we can predict impact distribution X
  with error bound Y”), the model risks becoming purely descriptive or retrospective.
  So the enhanced version is conceptually rich but mathematically slack in its foundations
  and philosophically vulnerable to metric-optimizing behavior that kills true genius.
