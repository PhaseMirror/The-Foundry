# ADR-0005: Constitutional Governance and Authority Protocol

## Status
Accepted

## Context
As the `legalese-scopist` evolves into the PhaseMirror Legal Edition, it requires a formal governance framework to ensure mathematical coherence and architectural legitimacy. Phase 0 requires a clear authority model to avoid the friction of premature decentralization.

## Decision
We establish the Ataraxia Phase 0 Constitution:
1. **Named Authority**: The Multiplicity Foundation is the sole constitutional author and root of trust.
2. **Amendment Protocol**: A lean 48-hour review cycle requiring explicit Archivum anchoring.
3. **Escalation Trigger**: Transition to M-of-N governance is triggered by the first external institutional partner or clinical pilot.
4. **Immutable Core**: The six L1-HC invariants are non-amendable bedrock.

## Consequences
- Every artifact has a verified origin and amendment path.
- Provides a stable foundation for the implementation of the MultiplicityCell and PEET drift engine.
- Ensures authority follows provenance in all legal/technical decisions.
