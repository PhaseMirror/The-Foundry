# ADR-011: L0-L Governs the Year-One Crate

## Status
Accepted [Initials: L.R.]

## Decision
L0-L (S = 0) governs the year-one UCC crate. ADR-010 (unmanifested = 0) governs transitional research trees but is insufficient for civic L0. The `lean/Core/phase_mirror_loop_scaffolds/` attic is permanently retired from the civic path.

## Consequences
- $S$ and $M$ must be reported separately. "Score 0" is deprecated as an aggregated metric.
- No triple-lock unification claims between Phase Mirror, UCC, and the loop.
