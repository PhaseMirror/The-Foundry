# ADR-PP-PIRTM-001: Adjacent Compilers

**Status:** Proposed

## Context
PrismPM and PIRTM define separate jurisdictions within the UOR Foundry framework. PrismPM dictates what a system is (catalogs, profiles, snapshots, and packaging law), while PIRTM dictates how a recursion may act (dynamical law, contractive tensors). "Proof-to-Executable" is currently a research paradigm, not a year-one closed loop; year-one operations depend on UCC closing typed objects and modeling portals. There is a need to clarify the boundaries between these compilers to ensure they remain adjacent and do not fuse, preventing any single compiler from appropriating Phase Mirror's five steps, WardMonitor's halt name (`SIG_GOV_KILL`), or UCC's authority.

## Decision
Keep PrismPM and PIRTM as distinct, adjacent compilers with separate jurisdictions of refusal. They must not be fused into a single closed-loop machine. 
*   **PrismPM** will refuse profiles that swallow C-controls or weaken constraints. 
*   **PIRTM** will refuse to compile or link any program that will not contract.
*   **UCC** will lead the year-one close (owning $\Delta \neq 0$).
*   **WardMonitor** retains exclusive ownership of the `SIG_GOV_KILL` interrupt for metabolic red conditions.
*   **Phase Mirror** will resolve precision questions by naming dissonance, not making pulse cuts.
The "five-step cryptographic envelope" (H2P) must be treated as experimental draft research ("H2P-draft") and must not share numbering or identity with the Phase Mirror loop. Unverified handwritten application code remains prohibited as an aspiration, but "hundian" (handwritten Rust with Kani harnesses) is permitted to maintain open-core usefulness.

## Consequences
*   PrismPM and PIRTM can independently reject an artifact ("two kinds of no"), but neither can approve an artifact on the other's behalf. Passing both does not mint "Certified" status or vote land.
*   The "Proof-to-Executable" paradigm remains isolated as research until a formal ADR binds H2P and ACE with test vectors.
*   The literal "five-step envelope" language will not be printed next to Phase Mirror, preventing public confusion.
*   The `SIG_GOV_KILL` label will be explicitly kept out of compiler refuse logs, ensuring it is only used by WardMonitor.
*   UOR Foundry preserves its civic invariants by keeping the process law (kiln) and dynamical law (clay) separate.
