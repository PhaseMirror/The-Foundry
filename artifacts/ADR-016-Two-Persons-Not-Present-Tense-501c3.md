# ADR-016: Two Persons, Not a Present-Tense 501(c)(3)

**Status:** Proposed

## Context
The source paragraph wants three true things: assets that cannot be extracted as private equity; a Wyoming nonprofit wrapper that can hold property without making every member a partner in a tort; a workshop that can close a state change with a receipt instead of a vibe. Those wants survive.

It also restores two recitals the capital packet already took out of present tense: that “the UOR Foundation operates exclusively as a registered 501(c)(3),” and that the Foundry “utilizes a DUNA” as if 100 consenting members were already on the public register. It then stacks a third body (“Foundation”) on top of the two persons the stack actually named, and gives the Phase Mirror an L0_HALT pin that other papers kept unbound. Expand the wants. Do not restore the recitals.

## Decision
1. **Maintain exactly two legal persons:** Citizen Gardens UNA (becoming a DUNA at 100 consenting members) and the Operator LLC. There is no LLC-to-charity graduation.
2. **"UOR Foundation" is a literary name:** It remains a literary and campus-proposal name, not a third legal entity. Title to land, if acquired, vests in Citizen Gardens UNA.
3. **501(c)(3) status is not a present-tense fact:** No IRS determination letter exists in the artifacts. The purpose-lock relies on civic L0-1 and the UNA/DUNA nonprofit form, prohibiting member dividend distribution.
4. **Retire present-tense claims:** Remove claims of being a "registered 501(c)(3)" and "we are a DUNA" from public materials until a determination letter is held or the 100-member floor is reached.
5. **Algorithmic governance supplements, not replaces:** A DUNA recognizing a ledger does not replace the human assembly. Civic L0-8 forbids credits from overweighting votes. The computation plane does not sit in the member's chair.
6. **Year-one product definition:** The year-one product is UCC as a service (a completion kernel), not "Certified compliance" or "Statutory proof". The Phase Mirror acts as a build-time diagnostic and runtime firewall. PWEH post-quantum secure evidence is not a year-one shop claim.

## Consequences
* Assets are protected from extraction as private equity through the UNA structure and civic L0-1, without requiring a 501(c)(3) determination letter.
* The 100-member count must be published monthly to track DUNA eligibility. Claiming DUNA status requires 100 consenting names on the public register.
* All wires for campus and kit must explicitly name the legal person and door.
* If a 501(c)(3) exemption is sought in the future, it must be filed under the existing person that holds the purpose, rather than inventing a third "Foundation" body to hold the letter.
* The Operator LLC holds the hosted Phase Mirror, managed-service license, seat revenue, and PMCP commercial terms, and does not hold the roster or take remainder interest in UNA land.
* The Portal SSP remains Modeled only, and external zero-knowledge audits for enterprises are deferred as research and workshop paths.
